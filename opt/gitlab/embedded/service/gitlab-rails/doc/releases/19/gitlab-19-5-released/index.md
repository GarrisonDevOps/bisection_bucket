On October 15, 2026, GitLab 19.5 was released with the following features.

We are excited to recognize [Dhairya](https://gitlab.com/DhairyaMajmudar), a Level 3 contributor,
as this month's [Notable Contributor](https://contributors.gitlab.com/notable-contributors)!

Dhairya contributed to offline transfer and Organizations support for import,
so that groups, projects, and their memberships can move between GitLab instances.

Before you upgrade to GitLab 19.5, review the
[upgrade notes](../../../update/versions/gitlab_19_changes.md#upgrade-to-195)
and the [deprecations and removals for GitLab 19.5](../../../update/deprecations.md#gitlab-195).
